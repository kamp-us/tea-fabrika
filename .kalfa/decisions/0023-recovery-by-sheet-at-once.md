# A lost device comes back by the sheet, which works at once and once

A person who loses every device comes back by the printed 12-word sheet, or by email on the cloud setup. A device whose keys did not come across goes to the recovery screen, never to a new founding, and never makes new keys by itself. A sheet lets one device in at once, with no wait and no passphrase, and is then spent; first the new device reads what the recovery will drop, and the signed `added` carries that read as `seen`, so it is refused if the person's keys changed since.
