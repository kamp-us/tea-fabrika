# The bottom is four layers: Ledger, Program, Transport, Identity

Everything in fabrika sits on four layers: the Ledger (storage), Program (what runs), Transport (the wire) and Identity (a key, and who it belongs to). Program also holds the run interface and long work: an answer that is still owed, with time kept by the place's own alarm. Each place's runner (a Durable Object, Node, a browser tab, a test) is an adapter, the way react-dom runs React.

## Consequences

Deletable content and blobs get no package of their own: Effect's `KeyValueStore` covers them. Traces stay in their own record objects, not in the ledger.
