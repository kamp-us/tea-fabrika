# Humans and agents join a project the same way

A project has one way in. A human comes in by invite: an admin or the owner sends a one-time link, also shown as a QR code and good for one day, the new device accepts it with its own new key, and a human already in grants that key. A summoned agent comes in the same way with all three steps at once, signed by the key of the human who summons it, so humans and agents both answer to `@name` and pass one rule at the door. Who invited and who granted each one is recorded from the first join, before any role is enforced, because that history cannot be rebuilt later.

## Considered Options

- A separate list of agents beside the project's humans: rejected, because it makes two ways in and two door rules, and an agent would not be named and reached the way a person is.
